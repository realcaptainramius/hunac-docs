Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

This policy explains what information Fleet Mate ("the app") collects, how it's used, and who it's shared with. It applies to the Fleet Mate iOS app and its companion backend.

Information we collect

To use Fleet Mate, you create an account with an email address and password. Beyond your account credentials, the app stores the vehicle and maintenance information you choose to enter, which may include:

  • Vehicle details such as make, model, year, VIN, and serial number
  • Maintenance records, odometer readings, and fuel logs
  • Receipts or photos you upload
  • Any messages you send to the in-app assistant (see "Third-party services" below)

We don't collect location data, contacts, or any information you don't directly enter into the app.

Where your data is stored

Fleet Mate's backend runs on infrastructure we operate and manage directly, rather than a third-party cloud platform. We don't sell your data, and we don't use advertising or analytics SDKs that track you.

Third-party services

Fleet Mate includes an optional in-app chat assistant that can answer questions about your vehicles. When you use this feature, the relevant portion of your vehicle data and your message are sent to Google's Gemini API for processing, subject to Google's API terms and privacy practices. This only happens when you actively use the chat feature.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide solely to operate the app's core features: storing your vehicles and maintenance history, generating reminders and summaries, and powering the chat assistant when you use it.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by emailing apps@hunacenterprises.com. We'll confirm once it's been removed.

Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections between the app and our backend. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

Children's privacy

Fleet Mate is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to apps@hunacenterprises.com.
