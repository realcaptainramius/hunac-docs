Common questions

How do I add a vehicle?

From the main screen, tap the add button and fill in the vehicle's details. You can add a VIN or serial number at creation time, or later from the vehicle's detail page.

How do I email documents to Fleet Mate?

Your account has its own Fleet Mate inbox address. Forward a receipt, invoice, or parts order to it. Fleet Mate reads the attachment, works out which vehicle it's for, and puts it in your Inbox on the web for you to review and confirm.

How do I import a CARFAX or AutoCheck report?

On the web, open the vehicle and click Import report on its History card, or use Upload Document and choose the report. Fleet Mate shows every owner, service visit, and inspection it found, and marks the ones that match records you already have. Untick anything you don't want, then click Import. Nothing is saved until you do.

Can Fleet Mate translate a manual in another language?

Yes. On the web, add the PDF or photos to the vehicle's Reference Library and click Translate under it. When it's done, click English to read each page beside the original. Fleet Mate's assistant can then answer questions from the manual and tells you which page it used.

Why does a Reference Library item have no summary?

Fleet Mate reads each saved file or link once and keeps a short summary. It skips files over about 18 MB, and it stops for the day when the daily AI limit is reached. For a large manual, use Translate: the summary is written from the translation when it finishes.

How do I export all my records?

On the web, open a vehicle and choose Download Report → Full Report with All Documentation. You'll get a ZIP with a PDF report and every original document stored for that vehicle: receipts, manuals, photos, history reports, and Reference Library files.

How do I delete my account or data?

To remove a single vehicle and all of its records, use Purge on the web app's Admin page (or Retire to hide it but keep its history). To delete your whole account, email us at the address above and we'll delete your account and associated data. See the Privacy Policy for details.
