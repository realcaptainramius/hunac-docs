Support

We're happy to help with bugs, questions, or feature requests.

Contact us

Email support@hunacenterprises.com and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

When reporting a bug, it helps to include: what you were doing, what you expected, what happened instead, and your device/iOS version.

Common questions

How do I add a vehicle?

From the main screen, tap the add button and fill in the vehicle's details. You can add a VIN or serial number at creation time, or later from the vehicle's detail page.

How do I delete my account or data?

Email us at the address above and we'll delete your account and associated data. See the Privacy Policy for details.
