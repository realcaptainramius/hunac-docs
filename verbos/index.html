How it works

  1. Add a word and look up its definition, part of speech, phonetic pronunciation, and example sentence — free, no account needed.
  2. Apply it by writing a real sentence with it. This is the step that makes it stick.
  3. Mastery is earned two ways: use a word in your own sentences 5+ times across 3+ different days, or correctly pick it out of 3+ different sentences in the Quiz. Either is real evidence you know it.
  4. Daily streaks and a weekly summary (with charts) show your progress.

Why it's different

Smart, private AI — on by default, never required

verbOS defaults to Apple's own on-device Apple Intelligence for richer definitions, sentence feedback, and quiz generation — completely private, instant, and free, with nothing ever sent to a server. If your device doesn't support it, connect your own Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini account instead — your key stays on your device. Every feature also works with no AI configured at all.

Voice and camera

Dictate your practice sentences instead of typing, with live on-device speech recognition that keeps listening right through natural pauses. Read-aloud buttons throughout the app use the system voice. On supported devices, scan a word straight off a printed page with your camera instead of typing it in.

Know how rare a word really is

Every word gets a rarity score from a real, offline word-frequency dataset — see instantly whether a word is Everyday, Uncommon, or genuinely Rare. Your Vocabulary Prestige Score rewards words you've actually mastered, weighted by rarity — not just words sitting unused in your list — and you can share it as a card with friends.

Syncs privately across your devices

Your word list syncs through your own private iCloud account across your iPhone and iPad — nobody else can see it, and there's no account to set up. A home screen widget shows today's focus words at a glance.

Built for iPhone, iPad, and Mac

A genuine iPad and Mac experience with a sidebar layout, not just a stretched iPhone screen — add a word, look it up, and apply it in a sentence from whichever device is in front of you, kept in sync.
