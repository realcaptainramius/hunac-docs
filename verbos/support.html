Common questions

Does verbOS need an internet connection?

No. Adding words, tracking mastery, quizzes, and streaks all work fully offline. A connection is only used for looking up a definition (via a free dictionary service) or for optional AI features.

How does the AI assistant work?

By default, verbOS uses Apple's on-device Apple Intelligence, so nothing leaves your device. You can optionally add your own API key for a cloud AI provider in Settings for richer results.

How do I sync my words across my devices?

verbOS can sync your word list across your own devices using Apple's iCloud, tied to your Apple ID. Check Settings → iCloud Sync to confirm it's active. If sync seems stuck, try exporting your words first (Settings → Export/Import) before changing any iCloud settings, as a precaution.

How do I back up or delete my data?

Use Settings → Export to save your words as a file. To remove your data, delete the app, or email us and we'll help with anything stored in iCloud.
