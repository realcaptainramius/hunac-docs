Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

This policy explains what information verbOS ("the app") collects, how it's used, and who it's shared with.

Information we collect

verbOS doesn't require an account or login. The words you add, their definitions and example sentences, and your practice/quiz history are stored directly on your device using Apple's on-device storage (SwiftData).

iCloud sync

If you're signed into iCloud, verbOS can optionally sync your word list across your own devices using Apple's private CloudKit database, tied to your Apple ID. This data is only accessible to you — we, the developers, cannot see or access it.

Third-party services

A few features send limited data to outside services, only when you actively use them:

  • Dictionary lookup: the word you're looking up is sent to Wiktionary's free API to fetch a definition, and separately to Datamuse's free API for synonyms. No account or personal information is attached to either.
  • AI features (enrich, feedback, examples, quiz backfill): by default, verbOS uses Apple's on-device Apple Intelligence — nothing leaves your device. If you choose to add your own API key for a cloud provider (Claude, OpenAI, or Gemini) in Settings, the relevant word or sentence text is sent directly from your device to that provider using your key, subject to that provider's own terms and privacy practices. This only happens if you've configured it.
  • Dictation and read-aloud: handled by Apple's on-device Speech and text-to-speech frameworks, following your device's own Siri & Dictation privacy settings.

We don't operate our own backend server for verbOS, don't collect location data or contacts, and don't use advertising or analytics SDKs that track you.

Data retention and deletion

Your data lives on your device (and your own iCloud account, if sync is enabled) for as long as the app is installed. Deleting the app removes local data; you can also delete verbOS's iCloud data via your device's iCloud settings. Contact us if you'd like help with anything and we're able to assist.

Children's privacy

verbOS is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to apps@hunacenterprises.com.
